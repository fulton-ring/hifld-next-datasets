# Child Care Centers

The draft source is the Day Care Centers layer (ID 9) of the U.S. EPA Office of Environmental Information's `OEI/ORNL_Education` SEGS MapServer on `geodata.epa.gov`. The service metadata identifies the data as produced by Oak Ridge National Laboratory's Geographic Information Science and Technology Group (HSIP Freedom Database) and procured for EPA through the Office of Environmental Information; the producer's own method paper (OSTI 1210152, DOI 10.3390/ijgi4031290) documents the ORNL national day care spatial database that contributes to the DHS HSIP program. The complete observed layer contained 115,036 point features in EPSG:4326 covering the 50 states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico, with the paper's four TYPE categories (Center Based, School Based, Head Start, Religious Facility).

This is the 2015 ORNL Freedom edition distributed by EPA. It is not the retired 2021-06-24 to 2022-03-01 HIFLD edition in the inventory row's DataLumos archive link: the HIFLD hub item returns HTTP 404, the DHS/HIFLD data.gov harvest record (MGMT-GMO-HIFLD-126087) has no distribution endpoint, and DataLumos/PASDA HIFLD rescue copies are ineligible pipeline inputs under project policy. No ORNL/DOE-hosted distribution of the day care database was found (DOE Data Explorer: zero records; ORNL GIST pages gone). Whether the retired HIFLD edition descends from the 2015 ORNL database, and whether the EPA distribution is an accepted successor source, remain unaccepted maintainer decisions.

The source captures all 28 native attributes and `OBJECTID` values 1-115036 in bounded 1000-record `resultOffset` pages ordered by `OBJECTID`, count and ID snapshots before and after paging, service and layer metadata, and response bodies, wire bodies, and request receipts. The transfer-limit flag is rejected on the final page so an incomplete population can never be sealed. Candidate generation retains the source CRS and point coordinates without reprojection or repair; the source `LATITUDE`/`LONGITUDE` geocode columns are preserved unchanged alongside the relocated on-entity geometry (the 2015 paper documents the on-entity relocation), with an exact float32 round-trip guard for the single-precision columns. Duplicate provider `ID` values are preserved rather than deduplicated. GeoParquet is the only candidate format; no display or lossy output policy is accepted.

Rights, source and edition acceptance, baseline comparison, schema/ID/coverage contracts, and release approval remain false. The registered refresh schedule is stopped.

## Contemporary reconstruction research, 2026-11-19

This section records research for [#958](https://github.com/fulton-ring/hifld-next-datasets/issues/958), not acceptance of a replacement for the edition above. The original [DOE/OSTI paper](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1210152) and [#36 method evidence](https://github.com/fulton-ring/hifld-next-datasets/issues/36#issuecomment-6737113081) describe harvesting state sources, excluding residential family/group care, classifying center/school/Head Start/religious facilities, and parcel geocoding with on-entity relocation. They do not establish later HIFLD membership or an identifier crosswalk. The EPA edition above remains historical evidence, not an accepted maintained national source.

[PR #1002](https://github.com/fulton-ring/hifld-next-datasets/pull/1002) already delivered the separate NY/WA pilot. Its dated 2026-11-16 extraction retained all 4,899 NY and 2,543 WA scoped rows/outcomes, including two surplus duplicate WA native-ID rows. Ten Census submissions per state yielded only 2 NY and 8 WA matched-result points. The [post-merge review](https://github.com/fulton-ring/hifld-next-datasets/pull/1002#issuecomment-6971547221) observed NY 4,898 / WA 2,543 later; these are dated observations, not fixed denominators. No pilot was rerun for this audit. Existing pilot paths and all false runtime gates remain unchanged.
