The capture observes the complete current population as witnesses: the producer's `returnCountOnly` and full OBJECTID roster are fetched before and after the ID-targeted pages, pages are derived at capture time from the observed roster (250 OBJECTIDs per page; 903 observed on 2026-11-11), and the shared `validate_capture_witness` gates stable before/after counts and unique IDs with exact page coverage. A plausibility band (500..2000 around the observed 903) quarantines a truncated or replaced service; the observed count and roster digest are recorded per capture, never pinned. All 59 attributes, original ID occurrences, literal strings/nulls and native EPSG:3857 Point WKB are preserved.
