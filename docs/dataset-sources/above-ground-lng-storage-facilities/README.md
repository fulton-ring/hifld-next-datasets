# Above Ground LNG Storage Facilities — blocked draft

The retained PASDA mirror and published `v1.0.0` candidate contain 13 Pennsylvania facilities only; the inventory covers the CONUS, Alaska, and Hawaii. This partial mirror is not a national source or accepted baseline.

Candidate leads: [EIA maps](https://www.eia.gov/maps/) and [EIA Energy Atlas](https://atlas.eia.gov/) identify official discovery portals, but the reviewed items are images or different subjects, not a facility feed. The [PASDA ZIP](https://www.pasda.psu.edu/download/hifld_fema/conus_clipped/Above_Ground_LNG_Storage_Facilities.zip) is a geographically clipped mirror with no verified EIA delegation. The [HIFLD FeatureServer](https://services1.arcgis.com/Hp6G80Pky0om7QvQ/arcgis/rest/services/Above_Ground_LNG_Storage_Facilities_gdb/FeatureServer/0) returned ArcGIS `400 Invalid URL`; it did not establish a complete layer. The [DHS crosswalk DOI](https://doi.org/10.3886/E241367V1) and [DataLumos archive](https://www.datalumos.org/datalumos/project/238493/version/V1/view) remain unverified; prior provider responses were HTTP 403 challenges.

Maintainers must decide whether to retain PA-only coverage or identify a verified national feed and explicitly approve a migration. Source authority, crosswalk, rights, baseline, and schema/ID contract remain unresolved; shared prerequisite [#417](https://github.com/fulton-ring/hifld-next-datasets/issues/417) remains blocking. No national continuity or release is approved.
