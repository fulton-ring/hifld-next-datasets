# Construction Minerals Operations historical USGS 2003 survey candidate

- Assigned dataset: USGS National Atlas *Construction Minerals Operations* layer (`minop2x020`, publication 200512, operations active 2003), identified by the pinned USGS metadata at https://thor-f5.er.usgs.gov/ngtoc/metadata/waf/atlas_legacy/minop2x020.xml.
- **Historical source:** the candidate input is a historical (2003) USGS source — *Active Mines and Mineral Processing Plants in the United States in 2003* (USGS National Minerals Information Center, FGDC publication 2005), a 2003 Minerals Information Team survey snapshot (`mineplant.zip`, MRDATA Last-Modified 2012-05-30). It is not a current or maintained census of construction-minerals operations, and no maintained successor of the retired National Atlas layer is published.
- Candidate input (USGS-owned, historical 2003 survey, explicit selection only): https://mrdata.usgs.gov/mineplant/mineplant.zip, with its FGDC metadata at https://mrdata.usgs.gov/metadata/mineplant.xml. The pipeline never fetches or polls by default and makes no authority, datum, schema/ID mapping, baseline-contract, rights, or release-acceptance claim.
- Original-release blocker (dated 2026-11-10): the retired layer's own distribution `http://dds.cr.usgs.gov/pub/data/nationalatlas/minop2x020_nt00019.tar.gz` returns 404, and `https://dds.cr.usgs.gov/` now redirects to the ERS login. The USGS EROS EarthExplorer National Atlas collection requires an ERS account registration (a human step) for download. No third-party mirror was substituted for the input.
- Retained comparison context only (never inputs): the existing published HIFLD v1 objects. They preserve the published identifier roster. Per the current source policy, the third-party Stanford preservation masters of the retired layer are no longer fetched or compared (issue #778); the archived successor-fidelity evidence they supported is not claimed by this revision.
- Scope of the candidate: the USGS package rows whose `COMMODITY` equals one of the eight assigned classes (ball clay, cement, common clay and shale, dimension stone, gypsum, mica, perlite, pumice) — 765 operations across 46 states. Datum claims are retained unresolved: the mineplant PRJ declares NAD 1927 while the retained USGS National Atlas legacy metadata declares NAD 1983; coordinates are unchanged as provided and no transformation is applied.
- Local job: `construction_minerals_preservation_reconstruction`; its monthly schedule is STOPPED.

The pipeline pins 10 objects: three USGS inputs (`mineplant.zip` 513,191 bytes, `6dabd3bb...`; `mineplant.xml` 26,366 bytes, `419629ed...`; atlas legacy metadata 19,769 bytes, `d68776d0...`) and seven existing HIFLD comparison objects, each with exact byte counts and SHA-256 digests.
