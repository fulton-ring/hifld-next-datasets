# Airport Area (USACE IENC) — blocked decision

The historical IENC `AIRPORT_AREA` layer 57 and published v1.0.0 candidate each contain 72 features. The candidate is unapproved: only 71 polygons match exactly; 43 matched rows have changed OBJECTIDs. Published `id` equals OBJECTID and cannot recover source `Feature_ID`, which is absent from the published schema. Published-only Gateway Air Tours Helipad and source-only Demopolis Municipal Airport establish a membership change despite equal counts/extents. No row-number or OBJECTID continuity mapping is approved.

The historical service is `https://ienccloud.us/arcgis/rest/services/IENC_Feature_Classes/AIRPORT_AREA/MapServer/57`; this charted IENC airport-area layer is not all U.S. airports (`AIRPORT_AREA_POINT` is a separate layer). The master and historical services share 72 `Feature_ID`s, but their OBJECTIDs differ for the 38/40 swap; their geometries are not exactly equal, and no tolerance is approved. The proposed mapping also observes 43 Information null/empty differences and 10 Source_Indication changes. The published item is `https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=6ee858a220a143a48c8d40a6cf0d3680`. Neither self-attribution nor the master item verifies independent USACE delegation or dataset release date. The original DHS crosswalk remains unverified; its DataLumos page and the official AGC landing returned provider HTTP 403.

Maintainers must accept or reject the exact pinned baseline, establish delegated source/release and crosswalk evidence, then approve a versioned membership, ID, schema and null-semantics migration. `Feature_ID` is the retained source identifier; the published candidate does not carry it. No source, baseline, contract, ingestion or release approval is implied. Keep **Blocked / Potential breaking change / Needs decision**.
